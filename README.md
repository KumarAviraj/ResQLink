# 🆘 ResQ Link

### *Connecting survivors to rescue teams when every second matters.*

ResQ Link is a proposed emergency-response system designed to help rescue teams **locate and efficiently recover survivors trapped during war and disaster situations**.

The system combines a **wearable ResQ Link band** with a digital rescue-center platform. The band is designed to send emergency signals and relevant survivor information to rescue teams, helping them prioritize and respond to people who need assistance.

## 🎯 Problem

During war and disaster situations, survivors may become trapped in damaged or isolated areas. Rescue teams can struggle to determine:

* Where survivors are located
* Who needs immediate assistance
* Who requires food or medical support
* Whether a survivor has already been located or rescued

This can make rescue operations slower and less organized.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

ResQ Link aims to create a connected system where a survivor's ResQ Link band can communicate important alerts to a centralized rescue platform.

The rescue platform can display:

* 📍 Survivor location
* 🆘 Emergency status
* 🍱 Food assistance requirements
* 🏥 Important medical information
* ⏱️ Last received signal
* 🚑 Rescue status

## 🖥️ Prototype

This GitHub project contains an interactive prototype of the ResQ Link rescue platform.

The prototype includes:

* 📊 Emergency response dashboard
* 👥 Survivor database
* 🆘 Emergency alert simulation
* 🍱 Food-request simulation
* 📍 Location-signal simulation
* 🚑 Rescue-team assignment
* ✅ Survivor recovery tracking

### Important

The physical wristband and its communication with the platform are **digitally simulated in this prototype**. The survivor information shown is fictional and is used only for demonstration.
